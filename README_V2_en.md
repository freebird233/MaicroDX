
# MaicroDX V2 Build Guide
<br>

1.	Before you begin, check that your tablet is an 11–13-inch model and no more than 220 mm wide. With an 11-inch tablet, there will be black borders on both sides and the upper display area will not be visible because the cabinet is too large for that screen.<br><br>
2.	Print all parts in the universal print profile on MakerWorld. Two engraving options are available. [View model](https://makerworld.com.cn/zh/models/1561531) <br><br>
<img width="600"  alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1ed6f3ef-a9dc-4e42-98a2-ad5a31cce627" />
<img width="290"  alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9fcad3f9-f75a-4de4-a90b-718ab4dec9be" />

3. To accommodate tablets up to 13 inches, this model is nearly twice as large as the previous version, and printing takes nearly twice as long. While it prints, you can start buying and preparing the hardware.

# Button Controls

Remove the housing from the 9-key keypad and take out the PCB.<br>
<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c978c0e6-fdb4-48b0-9297-3cd38f5da331"  width="400" > 
<br> The image above shows the PCB after the black switch sockets have been desoldered with a soldering iron. The solder pads should be clearly visible. <br>
 (Alternatively, solder the wires directly to the switch sockets for the same result.)<br>
<br>
<br> 
<br>

<img width="500"  alt="80c00f655ec31978e7290bde7d4e652" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/68191312-93d4-4be2-b3b4-c0a97311866e" />    <img width="300"  alt="80c00f655ec31978e7290bde7d4e652" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/13a27dda-3d21-4a31-9b79-2fc683db114c" />   
Connect the keypad PCB to your computer, download the software provided by the seller, and program the key assignments.
Consider turning off the LEDs to reduce power consumption.
<br>Configuration software: https://www.jianguoyun.com/p/DVZ2nxEQ1raWDRiXhOcFIAA
<br>
<br>
<br>

Install the low-profile red switches in the eight matching slots in the button ring. For the switches to fit correctly, their two pins must face toward the center of the ring.
<br>

The completed button control assembly should look like this.
<br> After routing the wires through the opening, secure them at the center with two-part epoxy to prevent external pulling forces from breaking them.
<br><br><br>
# Other Notes<br>
1. The keypad base (button ring base) connects to the cabinet using locating tabs. There is 1 mm of clearance in height, so apply 1 mm thick double-sided tape to the cabinet's locating tabs first.
<br><br><br>
